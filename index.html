Positions: community ADS-B feeds (airplanes.live / adsb.lol, ODbL), non-commercial use. Routes: adsbdb.com community route database — plausible, not authoritative. Elapsed / remaining times are estimates: remaining ≈ great-circle distance to destination ÷ current ground speed + 10 min descent/approach allowance; elapsed ≈ distance from origin ÷ ground speed + 6 min climb allowance. Not for operational use. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Carriers (callsign prefix)

Matched on the first three characters of the ADS-B callsign (ICAO airline code). Add affiliates as needed (e.g. FedEx feeder operators fly under their own codes).

Tail numbers

One per line. US N-numbers are converted to their transponder (ICAO hex) address automatically; you may also enter a 6-character hex directly. Optional label after a space, e.g. N12345 Flight school 172.

Relay

The public ADS-B feeds refuse direct requests from web pages (CORS), so the page goes through your Cloudflare Worker relay. Paste its URL, e.g. https://fleet-relay.yourname.workers.dev. Leave blank to try direct.

Refresh

Settings are kept in this browser only (nothing is sent anywhere or stored in the repo).
